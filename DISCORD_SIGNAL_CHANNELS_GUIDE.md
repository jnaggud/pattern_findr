## How Signals Work

### Signal Types

**LONG (BUY) Signal**
- Indicates the system has detected conditions favorable for upward price movement
- Generated when our ML models identify momentum exhaustion on the downside
- Entry price is noted at time of signal

**EXIT Signal**
- Indicates the system recommends closing the position
- Can be triggered by:
  - Opposite momentum signal detected
  - Take profit threshold reached
  - Stop loss threshold reached
  - Neutral zone crossing

### Signal Format

When a signal is posted, you'll see:

```
========================================
SIGNAL: LONG (BUY)
Ticker: SPY
Entry Price: $689.51
Time: 2026-01-08 09:30 AM EST
Model Confidence: High
========================================
```

```
========================================

Ticker: SPY
Exit Price: $695.23
Entry Price: $689.51
P&L: +0.83%
Reason: Opposite Signal
========================================
```

## Understanding Lookback Periods

### Why Multiple Timeframes?

Different lookback periods capture different market dynamics:

**1-Year Channels (SPY-1y, BTC-1y)**
- Trained on recent market conditions
- More responsive to current market regime
- Higher signal frequency
- Best for: Active traders, current trend trading

**2-Year Channels (SPY-2y, BTC-2y)**
- Balanced between responsiveness and stability
- Captures recent bull and bear cycles
- Moderate signal frequency
- Best for: Most traders, balanced approach

**5-Year Channels (SPY-5y, BTC-5y)**
- Trained across multiple market cycles
- More robust to regime changes
- Lower signal frequency, higher conviction
- Best for: Patient investors, swing trading

## FAQ

**Q: How are the signals generated?**
A: Our signals use machine learning models trained on historical price data, with over 100,000 parameter combinations tested to find optimal settings. The system identifies momentum exhaustion points where price direction is likely to reverse.

**Q: Why do different timeframes have different win rates?**
A: Longer timeframes (5y) have more training data, which can lead to more robust but sometimes less responsive signals. Shorter timeframes (1y) are more tuned to current conditions but have less historical validation.

**Q: What if I miss a signal?**
A: We recommend only entering on fresh signals. Chasing entries after a significant move can increase risk.

**Q: How should I handle overnight positions?**
A: SPY signals should be considered in context of market hours. BTC signals apply 24/7 as crypto markets never close.
